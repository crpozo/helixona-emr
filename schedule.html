How the alert works
A slot frees
Somebody cancels, no-shows, or a provider block is lifted. The schedule notices the moment it happens — nobody has to remember.
The list is matched
Type and subtype must match. Then the desired windows are checked, then how long they have waited. Only real matches are offered.
The slot is held, briefly
Whoever is offered gets 30 minutes before it passes to the next match. The slot stays visibly held on the calendar meanwhile.
