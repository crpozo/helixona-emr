Encounter states
Draft→ Reviewed→ Signed and locked→ Addendum
State changeWho may make itfrom framework v6What the engine does
Draft → Reviewed MA, nurse or PCC Prepares only. Suggestions stay pending and nothing dispatches. Pre-charting
Reviewed → Signed and locked Physician or extender Runs the guards below, then dispatches billing, labs, infusion, pharmacy and scheduling at once. Sign and lock
Care-team note → Co-signed Supervising physician Same guards as signing. The co-signature locks the note and dispatches it.
Signed and locked → Addendum Physician or extender The signed version stays intact; the addendum is a new version linked to it. Addendum
Signed and locked → Draft Nobody Not available in any module. A signed encounter is corrected by addendum, never reopened.
Guards that run before a state changes
Blocks No override
  • !An order conflicts with the allergy listAllergies
  • !An infusion note has no signed order and version to referenceIV order
  • !The signer did not evaluate the patient
  • !No supervising physician is available to co-sign
  • !The scribe tries to sign, order, or change a clinical list
A block is never shown as a dismissible warning. The state change does not happen until the cause is gone.
Warnings Override with a reason
  • !The assessment section is emptyAssessment
  • !The note repeats a prior visit almost word for wordQuality
  • !A co-signature is past the 48-hour policy
  • !Portal release is being held from the patient
Only a physician or extender may override, and only with a reason. Your name and the reason go to the audit store.
