Types and colour codes
The taxonomy behind the calendar — type, subtype, letters and colour, editable in one place
The colour code is data, not styling. A subtype owns its letters and its colour here, and every screen reads them — the block edge on the calendar, the chip in the appointment detail, the legend, and the treatment filter. Change it here and it changes everywhere at once.
Three channels, and they are the clinic’s, not ours. The background is the appointment type, the side bar is which treatment, and a faint watermark over the background is where the patient stands. The status symbols are drawn as inline SVG rather than emoji: an emoji renders in its own colours on top of a palette the clinic chose, and differently on every operating system. Anything marked no colour yet is waiting on the clinic and is drawn grey until then.
