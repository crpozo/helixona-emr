Types and colour codes
The taxonomy behind the calendar — type, subtype, letters and colour, editable in one place
The colour code is data, not styling. A subtype owns its letters and its colour here, and every screen reads them — the block edge on the calendar, the chip in the appointment detail, the legend, and the treatment filter. Change it here and it changes everywhere at once.
These are the clinic’s colours, not ours. They are lifted from the eCW menu unchanged, because the team already reads Mega C 50 as cream and Mito Boost as wine — a wireframe that “improves” them would teach the wrong thing. Ten of the twenty-three fall below 1.6:1 against white, which is why the calendar draws them as a filled swatch with a defining edge rather than a thin stripe, and prints the type texture in two tones so the shape reads on Cellular Boost’s black and Mega C 50’s cream alike. Anything the clinic has not given a colour for is marked as ours and is still open to change.
