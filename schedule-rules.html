Booking rules
What the calendar allows, warns about, or refuses — set once, applied to every booking.
Each rule chooses its own strength. Blocks refuse the booking outright. Warns lets the front desk continue on purpose — that is how the clinic overbooks a chair when it knows better. Allows turns the rule off without deleting it.
Double booking
Two appointments in the same provider slot
A provider cannot be in two rooms at once. Refuses the second booking and offers the next open start time.
applies to · Dr. Drannikov · Dr. Bakman · Brooke
Two infusions in the same chair
Chairs are physical — the second patient has nowhere to sit. Warns rather than blocks, because a short push can share the tail of a long drip when the medic agrees.
applies to · Chairs 1–4
One actor running two treatments at once
A medic can watch a running drip and start another, but cannot begin two at the same minute.
applies to · Nick · Bea · Juan · Nate · Wesley
Start times and duration
Medics start on the half hour
Thirty minutes is the start-time grid for the infusion team — it is not how long anything takes. A three-hour NAD+ still runs three hours; it just begins at :00 or :30.
applies to · Chairs 1–4 · lab draws
Use the patient's own duration, not the protocol default
When a patient has three or more past runs, booking reserves their real average. Maya Reyes books three hours twenty for NAD+, not three.
needs · 3 past runs of the same treatment
Infusion must finish before closing
A drip that would run past 6:00 PM cannot be started. The clinic closes and the chair must be clear.
clinic hours · 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM · closed Sunday
Clinical prerequisites
Intake must be complete before a first infusion
Consents, allergies and medications have to be on file before anything goes into a vein.
applies to · first infusion of any series
Readiness says pause
When the readiness engine returns Pause or Flare Recovery, escalating treatments stop until a provider clears it.
source · Readiness engine
Lab draw required before this protocol
High-dose IV vitamin C needs a current G6PD result on file.
applies to · IV vitamin C 25 g and above

Out-of-pocket max reached
Flag every appointment and tell the front desk
Once a patient has met their out-of-pocket max, everything after it is covered differently. Each new booking is flagged so nobody quotes the old price, and the front desk gets a notice the moment it happens.
Maya Reyes hit hers on Jul 14 · see her benefits
Membership booking priority
Members reach the calendar first
Prime chair times open to members before everyone else. Higher tiers see them sooner — Radiant at 14 days out, Gold at 10, Silver at 7, everyone else at 3.
