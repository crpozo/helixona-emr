What raises a task
The rules that turn something happening into somebody’s job
This page is the reason the task list can be trusted. Nobody maintains it by hand, so it does not rot. Every rule says three things: what has to happen, whose job it becomes, and how it closes itself — a rule with no closing condition creates work that piles up until people start ignoring the whole list.

Write a rule
Something happens → it becomes somebody’s job → it closes itself
1 What has to happen
A rule that fires on everything raises tasks nobody reads.
2 Whose job it becomes
A role, not a name — names go on holiday.
An instruction, not a topic. “Call Victor about his no-shows”, not “Victor Reyes”.
3 How it closes itself
A rule with no closing condition creates work that piles up until people stop reading the list. Choosing the last option is allowed, and it is the one to argue about.
