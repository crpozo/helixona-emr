HCOS Delivery Schedule

Helixona Clinical Operating System · Path from eClinicalWorks to full replacement
Prepared for Helixona leadership Updated August 2026 Single schedule of record Review the wireframe

1Delivery approach

HCOS goes live in three stages instead of a single cutover. Each stage puts working capability in front of the team while eClinicalWorks keeps running underneath, so patient care and billing continue without interruption at any point in the transition.

Two moves make this possible. First, eClinicalWorks migrates to Helixona’s own AWS environment, which gives HCOS live access to the complete clinical database while both systems coexist. Second, design and integration work run in parallel with that migration, so construction starts the moment the foundations are ready.

The starting point is further along than a blank page: the collaborative wireframe already covers all 12 modules across 174 screens — Modules A and B at full depth, plus Scheduling, Patients, Treatments & therapies and the patient journey — and the team annotates it directly. That wireframe is the scope reference this schedule builds.

4Phase detail

Phase 1 · Design and foundations

August to November 2026
  • Shared data model. The 22 canonical records every module uses — person/patient master, encounter, order, result, coverage, eligibility snapshot, plan of care, treatment administration, claim, audit event and the rest — already catalogued in the architecture review and registered on the wireframe’s Security module. Defined once, before construction, to prevent rework.
  • Wireframes for the remaining modules. The collaborative wireframe is already live at the review site with all 12 modules at high level — 174 screens, Modules A and B at full depth, plus deep passes on Scheduling, Patients, Treatments & therapies and the patient journey. Phase 1 deepens the remaining modules to buildable specification, reviewed with the team through the same feedback loop used for Modules A and B — wireframes complete by the end of October 2026, ahead of the rest of the phase.
  • eClinicalWorks migration to Helixona AWS. Follows the migration plan already presented (13 to 17 weeks). Result: full, continuous access to the clinic’s database while both systems coexist.
  • Integration work starts now, in parallel. The complete data export request to eClinicalWorks and the daily update feed begin against the current cloud system — not after the migration, alongside it.
  • DEA / EPCS certification begins. Electronic prescribing of controlled substances has a long external lead time and the pharmacy module depends on it, so the process starts here, three phases early. The FHIR R4 lab-feed certification (2 to 4 weeks, sandbox endpoint already available) is scheduled the same way, ahead of the labs build.
  • Billing rule validation. The billing team confirms every documented rule before any rule is written into software: infusion time thresholds, the Anthem J3490 ascorbic-acid unit methodology (1 unit = 500 mg), per-payer documentation routing (EDI 275 with the claim for Anthem and UHC; claim-number-first portal packets for Blue Shield and Cigna), and the standing policy that every recoupment is appealed.

Phase 2 · Platform core and first module

October 2026 to January 2027
  • Security, roles and the audit trail. The foundation every module inherits — the role and approval-rights matrix and the append-only enterprise audit store, both already specified screen-by-screen on the wireframe’s Security module.
  • One survey engine. Intake forms, clinical questionnaires and patient check-ins are a single engine with versioned definitions and explicit chart mappings — not three parallel builds. This is what ends the re-keying of questionnaire answers.
  • Real-time data capture. Activates once the self-hosted eClinicalWorks environment is live.
  • Detox Readiness Engine construction. The first production intelligence module: surveys, symptoms, labs, physician rules and treatment history in; Gentle / Standard / Advanced / Pause / Flare Recovery / Physician Review out. Fails closed to Physician Review.

Phase 3 · First go-live and clinical core

February to July 2027
  • February — Milestone 1. Readiness Engine, questionnaires and the Today View go live, reading current patient data from eClinicalWorks.
  • Clinical core construction continues. Encounters with a reliable ambient scribe and sign-and-lock that dispatches to labs, infusion, billing, pharmacy and scheduling at once; the infusion suite with Five Rights verification; orders with labs, infusions, prescriptions and therapies as first-class categories; intake; the caregiver portal; and scheduling with per-patient infusion durations and per-rule Block / Warn / Allow conflict strengths.

Phase 4 · Clinical operations live

August 2027
  • July is a shadow month. The team documents in HCOS while output is compared side by side with eClinicalWorks.
  • August — Milestone 2. Providers document in HCOS. The infusion suite records start and stop times and calculates billing units automatically, removing the manual entry step that causes claim corrections today.
  • Charges flow to billing. Through the current clearinghouse process (Waystar), unchanged — the billing team’s workflow does not move until Phase 5.

Phase 5 · Full replacement

September to December 2027
  • Remaining modules. Revenue cycle with the configurable rules engine and the out-of-network documentation packets (FAIR Health benchmarks, plan-governance classification); labs on the certified FHIR feed; pharmacy with certified electronic prescribing; patient communications; reporting and administration.
  • Historical data verified, then archived. Historical data is verified inside HCOS, then eClinicalWorks moves to a low-cost read-only archive.
  • December — Milestone 3. HCOS is the clinic’s single system.

6Assumptions

  1. The migration completes inside the 13 to 17 week window in the migration report.
  2. Module scope follows the approved wireframes — the live review site is the scope reference.
  3. No patient data enters development environments at any point.
  4. Dates are reviewed monthly and this page is updated as the single schedule of record.
