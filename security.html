Where each piece of information lives, who writes it, and which screens are only views of it
Information exists once, with many controlled views. A screen that shows a record is a view of it. A screen that keeps its own copy, applies its own rules, or lets the wrong role change an approved decision is a defect — not a variation.

eCW ownership and cutover
Which system writes each domain today, and what has to be true before HCOS takes it over
Until a domain activates in HCOS, eCW is its only writer and HCOS reads. Two systems never write the same domain at the same time. Cutover happens one domain at a time, and the rule in force is stated on the screen where the data appears.
