Staff admin
Admin only. Creates people, sets their working time, and decides how fine the booking grid is. Everything the capacity page counts starts here.
Working time is what makes someone a resource. A person without a working-time template is on the payroll and off the board: no shifts, no capacity, nothing bookable against them. Set the template once; the calendar copies it week to week and the office manager only touches exceptions.

Slot timing
How fine the booking grid is, per column. Admin only, and it only changes future days.
The grid is not the appointment length. A 15-minute grid on Dr. Bakman lets a chiropractic visit start at 8:15; it does not make a 30-minute visit shorter. Finer grids mean more starts to offer and more gaps to leave — which is why the clinic's day columns are on 30 and the MA's on 15.
