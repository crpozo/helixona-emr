The Work of Healing — how it is governed
From the product concept, initial release. Reviewer context, not product UI.
Who gets it, and when
Care pathManagement modelRelease
Complex Chronic CareComprehensive Care (always)MVP — the design population
Chronic CareComprehensive or CollaborativeExpansion 1
Collaborative specialty careCollaborative — e.g. oncology support, high-dose vitamin C under a referring physicianExpansion 1
Lifetime Health & WellnessDefined by the programExpansion 2 — habits, function, wearables
The care path says why the patient is here; the management model says who directs the care. Referral source never decides eligibility. These are the two axes the architecture review said never to mix.
The Helixona voice — samples the library starts from
Routine progress · automaticYou completed several important parts of your plan this week. Every one of those steps represents time and effort you invested in your healing.
Return after a difficult period · automaticIt is wonderful to see you reconnecting with your plan. Returning is an important step, and your care team is here beside you as you build your rhythm again.
Care-guide outreach · a personHi Priya, we’ve missed hearing from you and wanted to check in. How are you doing? Is there anything we can do to support you right now? Can we jump on a call and see what might be helpful? We’re here for you.
One consistent voice; patients are not asked to pick a motivational personality. Name the work, ask how they are, offer a way to connect, keep it proportionate. Every patient-facing line passes a voice review before release.
AI may · AI may not
MayMay not
Identify progress against the patient’s own baselineCreate or change a plan, pace, threshold, hold or escalation rule
Draft a specific, warm recognition from approved dataPresent an inference as clinical fact, or claim a treatment caused improvement
Detect changes meeting clinician-defined rules and show the eventsDecide on its own that engagement is adequate or inadequate
Summarise barriers and route them per the approved workflowSend sensitive clinical or emotional messages outside the automation boundary
Prompt the care guide when human recognition may be meaningfulCompare or rank patients, or use purchasing as a measure of progress
Every AI observation keeps its source events, time, rule or model version, review status and human edits. Staff can always see why a prompt appeared.
Not in this product, on purpose

No leaderboards. No comparisons between patients. No tangible prizes, purchased achievements or rewards for consuming more services. No streak that resets to zero. The Rewards tab (points, tier ladder, catalogue) is superseded by this and is kept only until the team decides what, if anything, survives from it.
